# AI Surf Coach (Clip Review) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A Coach tab on waveslave.tw where a signed-in Taiwan surfer sends 8 to 12 close-up key frames of one move and gets
back what went well, one main fix and a drill, drafted by Claude and approved by Bryan. It ships hidden (developer mode only,
`PUBLIC = false`) and asleep (no AI key) until Bryan's setup and his "go live".

**Architecture:**
- **Backend:** a second SQLite Durable Object class, `Coach` (`chat/src/coach.js`, name `taiwan`), in the chat's Worker. The
  Worker checks the sign-in through a new Room RPC, `whoIs`, then hands the request to `Coach.route()` over RPC. The AI call and
  the alert emails run in Coach's alarm, never in a surfer's request.
- **Pure logic:** `chat/src/coach-rules.js` (time, money, error sorting, frame checks) and `chat/src/coach-guide.js` (lists,
  guide, reply schema, request builder, reply checks), tested under plain Node.
- **Front end:** one new IIFE, `web/coach.js`, loaded with `defer` before `chat.js`. It cuts the key frames on the phone, keeps
  progress in IndexedDB and borrows chat.js's LINE sign-in through `CHAT.signIn("coach")`.

**Tech Stack:** Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects (SQLite, RPC, alarms), Wrangler 4.148.0, Vitest 4.1.11 with
`@cloudflare/vitest-pool-workers` 0.22.0, `node --test`, vanilla JS, Playwright 1.56.1 (Chromium) at 390×844, ffmpeg.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-ai-coach-design.md` (approved by Bryan, 2026-10-23). Read it first: every value
this plan doesn't spell out is in the spec, and the spec wins where they differ.

## Global Constraints

- **Repo and branch:** `ragingsurf/waveslave`, branch `claude/project-thread-ofjgew`. Don't touch the forecast-vs-buoy log
  (`log*/`, `logstore.sh`), `refresh.sh`'s data steps, or other countries.
- **Page safety:** the page works exactly as today when `coach.js` is missing, `window.__chatUrl` is `""`, chat.js is missing or
  the backend is down. coach.js never throws into the page and asks the backend nothing while `on()` is false.
- **Globals:** coach.js adds only `window.COACH = {dev, relang, spot, open}`. `window.CHAT` becomes exactly
  `{dev, relang, signIn, spot, token}`.
- **Escaping:** no `innerHTML`, `outerHTML`, `insertAdjacentHTML` or `document.write` in coach.js, and no `data-zh`/`data-ja` on
  coach elements.
- **Languages:** coach.js carries `en` and `zh` (Traditional, Taiwan usage); any other `LANG` falls back to `en`. chat.js's
  changed texts are written in `en`, `zh` and `ja`.
- **Surfer-facing words:** "key frames" (關鍵影格), "frame by frame" (逐格). Never picture, photo, image, still, snapshot or
  screenshot (照片, 圖片, 相片, 截圖, 靜態畫面), and never a claim that the coach watched the video or the whole ride.
- **Privacy line, exact:** "Only key frames are sent. Your video never leaves your phone." / 「只會傳送關鍵影格，你的影片不會離開你的手機。」
- **Identity:** only `uid` and `sub_hash` are stored. `metadata.user_id` is `sha256b64u("coach:" + sub_hash)`. No names, LINE
  details or frames in any email.
- **Secrets:** never in the repo, the page or logs. New Worker secrets `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, `RESEND_API_KEY`; test stand-ins
  only in `chat/vitest.config.mjs` and the browser tests' `dev.env`.
- **Versions:** no new npm packages; `compatibility_date` stays `"2026-10-21"` (tests run at `"2026-08-15"`).
- **Logging:** only an error's name (`console.error("coach: …", err?.name ?? "error")`); never a token, frame, prompt, reply or key.
- **Comments:** plain English, Bryan's decisions dated "(Bryan, Oct 22)" or "(Bryan, Oct 23)", matching the repo.
- **No model identifier** in commit messages or the PR text; it appears only as `COACH_MODEL` in `wrangler.toml`.
- **Commits** end with the trailers the session gives.
- **Vitest clocks:** every Vitest Coach test sets Coach's (and Room's) clock in 2030 or later, as `me.spec.mjs:22` does, so no
  alarm fires by itself mid-test. 2026 dates appear only in the plain-Node `coach-rules.test.mjs`. Every fetch mock throws on a
  `COACH_API_URL` or `COACH_MAIL_URL` call it wasn't told to answer.
- **Shapes used everywhere:**
  - `who = {uid, sub_hash, role}`; Coach answers `{status, json}` or `{status, jpeg: Uint8Array}`.
  - A box is `{x, y, side}`: integers, top-left corner, in the video's displayed pixels (`videoWidth` × `videoHeight` as the
    browser reports them; coach.js never rotates). The API and the `frames` row carry it as `[x, y, side]`.
  - Stored statuses: `uploading`, `queued`, `calling`, `drafted`, `published`, `rejected`, `failed`. The surfer's list maps
    `queued|calling|drafted` → `reviewing`, `published` → `ready`, `rejected|failed` → `not_reviewed`.
  - Error codes and statuses: `auth` 401; `admin`, `agree`, `banned`, `blocked` 403; `not_found` 404; `in_progress`,
    `not_uploading`, `missing_frames`, `state`, `redo_limit` 409; `too_big` 413; `full_today`, `no_reviews_left` 429;
    `invalid`, `too_few_frames`, `bad_frame` 400; `break` 503; `money` 503 with `until` (ms).
- **Commands:**
  - Backend: always `cd chat && …`. Socket-using files run with `npx vitest run --no-isolate --max-workers=1 <files>`.
  - Browser tests: `S=$(mktemp -d)`; once, `mkdir -p "$S/live" && curl -fsSL -o "$S/live/index.html"
    https://ragingsurf.github.io/waveslave/`; after every change to `template.html`, `web/head.html` or `web/*.js`,
    `python3 -I tests/map/tool.py page "$S/live/index.html" template.html null "$S/page.html"`. Every
    `node tests/chat/<name>.test.js` and `node tests/coach/<name>.test.js` takes `"$S/page.html"`.
  - Scratch output: `"${TMPDIR:-/tmp}/…"`.

## Rulings (where the spec leaves a choice)

- **R1. Stub helpers** live in `chat/src/stubs.js`. Room reaches Coach through `this.coachStub()`, Coach reaches Room through
  `this.roomStub()`; tests replace those methods as they replace `now()`.
- **R2. One RPC entry for routes:** `Coach.route({method, path, who, body})`. The Worker reads the body as text and never
  parses a frame.
- **R3.** Room's sync `isBanned(account)` (`room.js:851`) is renamed `bannedSub` for its callers (:739, :833); the public
  `async isBanned(sub_hash)` is the RPC and counts itself in the meter.
- **R4. `/admin/where`:** Room's `where()` awaits `this.coachStub().where()` and answers `room colo=NRT\ncoach colo=KIX\n`, or
  `coach colo=?` when Coach fails.
- **R5. `/me/export`** answers 503 `{error: "coach"}` when `Coach.exportFor` throws, like `DELETE /me`.
- **R6. The 24-month clean-up** is async and pages by `id`, so an account Coach didn't confirm is skipped, not picked again.
- **R7. Test seams:** `[vars]` `COACH_API_URL = "https://api.anthropic.com/v1/messages"` and
  `COACH_MAIL_URL = "https://api.resend.com/emails"`, like `LINE_TOKEN_URL`.
- **R8. Days:** "10 a day" counts Taiwan calendar days; the usage guard counts UTC days (Cloudflare's).
- **R9. The hourly email** is a meta `digest_due` time on the alarm's earliest-of list.
- **R10. Self-stop:** a key or model error sets meta `stopped`. Settings shows "Coach stopped" with **Start again**, which
  clears `stopped` (and `paused`).
- **R11. "still"** counts as a banned word only as `stills` or `<this|the|that|each|every|a> still`.
- **R12. Two tabs uploading for one account:** the newer start deletes the older upload; the older tab restarts once, and on a
  second `not_found` shows "Couldn't send. Tap to try again."
- **R13. Test clips are VP9 WebM** made by ffmpeg (Playwright's Chromium has no H.264 or HEVC decoder).
- **R14. Bryan's example** (Task 1) waits for his OK: until then the guide has no worked example and the signed-out view shows
  Task 1's draft; Task 22 puts in the OK'd text and images.
- **R15. Browser checks:** the flows, resumes, backend error codes and Drafts actions run against `wrangler dev` with fake LINE
  and a fake AI (`tests/coach/app.test.js`), as the spec asks. Only what the real backend can't produce (a `<video>` error,
  IndexedDB throwing, cut network, guards and globals) runs offline in `tests/coach/shell.test.js`.
- **R16. Not-surf and flagged replies:** not-surf → `rejected`, `hold = 1`, count kept; flagged (refusal, sexual, under 18) →
  frames deleted, `rejected`, `hold = 1`, `people.blocked = 1`, count kept. The surfer sees "We couldn't review this clip." A
  held review stays in Drafts' `waiting` until Bryan's Give back or Ban (not-surf; its frames also go after 7 days, ending the
  hold) or Clear or Ban (flagged).
- **R17. Withdraw** needs a note and works on a published review or a reported one.
- **R18. The email's link** is `https://waveslave.tw/?coach=drafts`, which opens the Drafts list (asking the developer PIN first,
  as chat.js's `?chat` return does).
- **R19. LINE on Android:** the "Open in your browser" link is the page address with `openExternalBrowser=1` added.
- **R20. The footer** follows `reviews.checked` (1 once Bryan approved or edited it), not the current autopilot setting.

## Review Focus

1. **A portrait or rotated phone clip.** Boxes and crops use `videoWidth`/`videoHeight` as reported, every frame is 784 × 784
   with the surfer inside, and no canvas is ever bigger than 784 × 784 or a quarter of the frame. Test owner: Task 19 (portrait
   pixels, a rotation-tagged WebM, canvas sizes); Bryan's HEVC clip in iPhone Safari is a go-live check (Task 14).
2. **The same account in two tabs.** One review on the server, and the older tab ends on "Couldn't send. Tap to try again."
   after one silent restart (R12). Test owner: Task 19.
3. **The Taiwan month changes between start and send.** Started 23:59 on 31 October (Taiwan), sent 00:01 on 1 November → it
   counts in November and the panel names November, whatever the phone's clock says. Test owner: Task 7 (count) and Task 20
   (message).
4. **Bryan's edits that are empty, huge or hold markup.** Each edited field is 1–1,000 characters after trimming, else
   `invalid`; nine full 中文 fields fit; markup shows as text. Test owner: Task 10 (backend), Task 20 and 21 (render).
5. **The credit month turns over while reviews wait on a money rest.** At 08:00 Taiwan on the 16th waiting reviews run, their
   cost counts in the new month, and no count is given back twice. Test owner: Task 9.
